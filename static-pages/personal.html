A few words about me

The real me.

By day, I build things with code. Beyond the screen: cricket, familiar faces, a good meal, and time enough to notice the day.

I am Shanmugavel Suriya: born in Avarampatti, Rajapalayam; shaped by the south; still curious about what comes next. I believe the best memories are rarely announced. They happen around a table, along a long walk, or in the company of people who let you be entirely yourself.

A small portrait

Where I began

A healthy mix of gym sessions and a Netflix evening has its place, too.

A letter to the life ahead

Room for two.

My big dream is a whole, meaningful life: happiness, peace, laughter, and being together through the ordinary days as much as the grand ones.

I hope to leave people a little happier for having crossed my path, to build real connections, and to keep choosing quality over noise. I would love to meet someone kind, funny, and genuine: a person who laughs with me, speaks honestly, and believes in making a good life side by side.

At the heart of it: honest words, loyalty, family close, and laughter in the ordinary days.

A little adventure. A little chaos. A lot of heart.
